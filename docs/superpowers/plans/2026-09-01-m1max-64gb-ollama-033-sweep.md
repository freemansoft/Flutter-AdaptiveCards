**Goal:** Re-measure all fifteen models on this Apple M1 Max / 64 GB against Ollama **≥ 0.33.1**, beginning with `qwen3.5:9b`, to test whether the one model that ran _faster_ on the smaller M5 did so because of the runtime rather than the hardware — then propagate every figure that moves into `ModelBehavior.md`, the five blog drafts, and the changelog.

## The experiment this plan exists to run

`qwen3.5:9b` is the model that improved on the smaller machine, and it is the
model to measure first. Its two recorded runs:

| Configuration           | Median  | Full sweep | Stalls | Seeded coverage   | Unaided coverage      |
| ----------------------- | ------- | ---------- | ------ | ----------------- | --------------------- |
| M1 Max / 64 GB, 0.32.14 | 6903 ms | 34 min     | 0      | 20 cold / 19 warm | 17 cold / 17 warm     |
| M5 / 16 GB, 0.33.1      | 5648 ms | 29 min     | 0      | 20 cold / 19 warm | **19** cold / 17 warm |

Two independent signals separate runtime from hardware, and the second is the
stronger one because it does not depend on timing at all:

1. **Latency.** The M5 is memory-bandwidth-poorer than the M1 Max (~150 GB/s against ~400 GB/s), so on hardware alone the M5 should be slower. It is faster, by 1.22x. If the runtime is responsible, this host on 0.33.x should come in **below 6903 ms**.
2. **Behavior.** In the **unaided** probe, exactly four calls differ between the two archives — `choice2` and `choice5`, both samples, cold condition:

   ```
   0.32.14 (M1 Max):  no-input: got {TextBlock} want {Input.ChoiceSet}
   0.33.1  (M5):      Input.ChoiceSet, TextBlock
   ```

   That is what lifts unaided cold-start 17 → 19. The **seeded** probe shows
   **zero** differing calls out of 100 across the two archives, so this is not
   general nondeterminism — it is two specific cases, reproducing across both
   samples at `t=0` greedy.

A runtime change can move behavior as well as speed, and those four calls are a
cleaner fingerprint than any latency figure. If this host on 0.33.x reproduces
the `Input.ChoiceSet` labels on `choice2`/`choice5`, the runtime is established
as the cause and the hardware explanation is out.

**Order of work follows from this.** `qwen3.5:9b` is Task 4 and runs alone, in
about 34 minutes, before the remaining fourteen models are swept in Task 5. The
answer arrives in half an hour rather than after a six-hour sweep, and if it is
negative the rest of the plan can be re-scoped rather than run blind.
